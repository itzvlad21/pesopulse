    How Credit Cards Work

    Credit cards track money you owe. When you log an Expense using a Credit Card, your "Owed Balance" goes up, and your "Available Credit" goes down.

    When it is time to pay your bill, go to the Dashboard and log a Transfer from your Bank (e.g., BPI) to your Credit Card. This will pay off the owed balance without messing up your total expenses!
